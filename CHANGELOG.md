# Changelog

## v0.4.0

The mod moves into the game's own menus, so no more UMM window, plus some quality-of-life fixes.

Menus:

- Pause > Multiplayer > Events: invite to S.K.A.T.E., rematch, set up and run races
- Pause > Settings > Multiplayer Events: letter colors, gameplay options, your word and blocked players
- Invite someone to S.K.A.T.E. straight from their page in the Players list
- Block players from a list in Settings
- Works with a controller, like the rest of the menus

Quality of life:

- Starting a game or race closes the menu
- Stop, leave, cancel and block ask for a second press, so no more stopping by accident
- Remove the last checkpoint from the menu, or press X while placing to undo
- Opening checkpoint placement no longer drops a post on its own

## v0.3.0

New Race mode (preview):

- Place checkpoints anywhere, set the laps, open a lobby for the room to join
- Only people who join take part, and it stays out of everyone else's events
- 3, 2, 1, GO countdown, a beacon over your next gate, and a live ranking
- Bail and you respawn at your last checkpoint
- Rematch the same course, or leave whenever

Game of S.K.A.T.E.:

- Pass your turn without bailing (hold dpad left)
- Spectate the other player while you wait (hold dpad right)
- Clearer HUD: whose turn, the trick to match, what you landed, and a turn-change sound
- Second try on your last letter
- A tiny manual before a pop no longer changes your trick
- Trick name fix, and the HUD scales with resolution
