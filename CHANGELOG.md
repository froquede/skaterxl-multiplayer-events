# Changelog

## v0.3.0

New Race mode (preview):

- Place checkpoints anywhere, set the laps, open a lobby for the room to join
- Only people who join take part, and it stays out of everyone else's events
- 3, 2, 1, GO countdown, a beacon over your next gate, and a live ranking
- Bail and you respawn at your last checkpoint
- Rematch the same course, or leave whenever

Game of S.K.A.T.E.:

- Pass your turn without bailing (hold dpad left)
- Spectate the other player while you wait (hold dpad right)
- Clearer HUD: whose turn, the trick to match, what you landed, and a turn-change sound
- Second try on your last letter
- A tiny manual before a pop no longer changes your trick
- Trick name fix, and the HUD scales with resolution
