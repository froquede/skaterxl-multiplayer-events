# Changelog

## v0.3.0

New Race mode (preview). Place checkpoints wherever you want, pick the number of laps,
and open a lobby for the room to join. Only people who join take part, and it stays out
of everyone else's events. There's a 3, 2, 1, GO countdown, a beacon over your next gate,
and a live ranking. Bail and you respawn at your last checkpoint. Rematch the same course
or leave whenever.

There's also a batch of Game of S.K.A.T.E. changes. You can pass your turn without bailing
(hold dpad left), and spectate the other player while you wait (hold dpad right). The HUD
is clearer about whose turn it is, the trick to match, and what you actually landed, plus
a sound on turn changes. You get a second try on your last letter. A tiny manual before a
pop no longer changes your trick. Fixed a trick name glitch, and the HUD scales with
resolution now.

## v0.2.0

Invites plus auto-detection of who else has the mod, a rematch button, and a custom word
for the host (like SK8).

## v0.1.1

Bug fixes and S.K.A.T.E. tweaks.

## v0.1.0
