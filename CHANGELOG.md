# Changelog

## v0.3.0

### New: Race (preview)
- A checkpoint race mode. Place gates anywhere, set the number of laps, and invite the
  room to a lobby — only players who join take part, and it never disturbs other events.
- Everyone races through the gates in order with a **3‑2‑1‑GO** countdown, a beacon
  marking your next gate, and a live ranking (finishers by time, then who's furthest).
- Bailing respawns you at your last checkpoint. **Rematch** re‑runs the same course, and
  you can **Leave** any time.
- This is a preview — feedback welcome.

### Game of S.K.A.T.E. improvements
- **Pass your turn** without bailing (hold D‑pad left).
- **Spectate your opponent** while you wait (hold D‑pad right); it returns you
  automatically when it's your turn.
- Clearer HUD: whose turn it is, the trick to **Match**, what **You** just landed, plus a
  sound on each turn change.
- **Second chance on match point** — two tries at the final, game‑losing letter.
- A tiny **manual before a pop** no longer changes your trick.
- Fixed a trick‑name display glitch, and the HUD now scales with your resolution.
